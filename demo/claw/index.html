      For marketers: campaign settings

      The marketer sets the odds and stock for each prize, so the promo can't go over budget. Aim nudges the grip a little, so it still feels like skill. Anyone who wins nothing in their three tries gets a 5% voucher, so every player has a reason to visit the store.

      Live stats

      Stats are saved on this device only. In a live campaign they would go to the analytics tool, and each voucher code would be matched at the till.
