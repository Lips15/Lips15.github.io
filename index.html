Monthly reporting cut from three days to 30 minutes with AI

Industry
Fashion retail
My role
Built and run the reporting workflow
Data
Meta Business Suite, Meta Ads Manager, Xiaohongshu
Readers
Business owner and management

Challenge

Every month management needs two reports: organic social media across Facebook, Instagram and Xiaohongshu, and Meta paid ads. Each one meant pulling numbers from several dashboards, comparing them with earlier months and explaining what changed in plain language. Done by hand, the two reports took about three days every month.

What I did

Built a reporting workflow with Claude so both reports come out in the same structure, with the same definitions, every month. The social media report separates organic from paid by subtracting ad-driven views, compares the last four months, picks the best and worst post on each platform with a reason, and ends with next month's to-do list. The ads report splits spend by purpose, tracks cost per result against the previous three months, explains what changed and lists the decisions the owner needs to make.

Because every month is laid out the same way, patterns show up that are easy to miss. From June to September, sales ads using an image with the price brought in enquiries far more cheaply than video, so sales ads moved back to images and video was kept for follower growth.

Results

  • 30 minfor both monthly reports, down from 3 days
  • 2.8×cheaper enquiries from image ads than video
  • 8–10×organic reach for question posts vs a typical post

Meta Ads report generator: one export in, a full analysis out

Type
Reusable reporting tool
My role
Designed the report logic, built with Claude
Input
Meta Ads Manager CSV export
Output
Interactive HTML report

Challenge

Ads reports often stop at totals. Campaigns with different goals get judged on the same cost, a few small ads distort the rankings, and creative fatigue is noticed only after the budget is spent.

What I did

Built a generator that turns one Ads Manager export into a six-part report: summary, spend share, daily trends, cost per result, best and worst ads, and next month's actions. It works out costs separately for purchases, conversations and leads, compares each ad only with ads chasing the same result, and leaves out ads too small to judge. Recommendations follow set rules: pause ads that spent without results, replace ads costing more than twice the average, refresh creative when cost per result climbs while click-through falls, and raise budgets on winners by about 20% every three to four days so they don't restart Meta's learning phase.
